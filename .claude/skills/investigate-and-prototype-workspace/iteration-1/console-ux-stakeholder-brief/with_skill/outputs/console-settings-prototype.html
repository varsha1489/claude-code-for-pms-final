Console UX backlog — mock, not shipped

Accessibility & alerts

A mock of the top three console asks from the Sep 2026 redesign interviews: status-badge readability (Ambrose, Aunt Dot), dark mode (Kip), and distinct per-responder alert colors (Kip). Toggle anything below — the mock console beneath reacts live. Grounded in 00-rook/feedback/interviews/; built as a discussion aid, not tested with real handlers yet.

Appearance

Dark mode
Kip, every interview he's given: "It's 11pm... the console is this wall of white light in the middle of my face." Tried the OS color-filter workaround and called it unusable. An actual theme, not a filter.
Status text size
Ambrose: "cannot tell engaged from available without leaning in." Aunt Dot, independently: can't tell if something changed from a normal standing distance. Same element, two unprompted reports.

Alerts

Distinct color/tone per responder
Kip, managing two responders: "both just go 'bing' and I have to actually look to know which one." Auto-assigned when a responder is added — no manual setup, since this gets used at 2am.
Warn on filter reset
Ambrose: filter persistence (shipped in 4.2) has silently reverted to default twice; he didn't notice for 15 minutes. "I'd rather it warned me the filter had reset than simply reset it." This is a reliability bug in a shipped feature, not a new design ask — route to engineering either way.

Coming next proposed, not built

Marked here on purpose rather than left out silently — "flagged" isn't the same as "acted on," and that gap showed up more than once in earlier prototype feedback too.

PROPOSED
Notify the handler too, not just the responder's phone

Aunt Dot: she hears the offer arrive on the responder's phone through a ceiling, not on her own screen. A second notification recipient is a bigger feature than a toggle — needs scoping before it goes on a release.

PROPOSED

Requisition approval queue has no real prioritization, field failure reports get no feedback loop, catalog search has no category filters. Not console, not Dispatch — passing to whoever owns Supply.
