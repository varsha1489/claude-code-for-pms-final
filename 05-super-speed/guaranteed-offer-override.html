Why we made this: The automatic version isn't built yet. This lets a handler do the same thing by hand, today, for the 4 people who need it most.

Why 7 days? That's about as long as the quietest real stretch went before a handler reached out directly asking if something was wrong. Short enough to catch it early, long enough that a normal slow week doesn't set it off.

If they miss it or say no, they don't lose their shot — but it's not unlimited. The guaranteed job gets a longer window to answer than normal — three times as long, since this one actually matters — and if they still miss it or turn it down, the next matching job goes to them too. After 2 tries, it doesn't just keep waiting forever: it's flagged for their handler to step in directly, instead of silently sitting open with no end in sight.
